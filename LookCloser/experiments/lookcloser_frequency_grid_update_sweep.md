## Insights

- Carry forward `grid_update_interval=512` and `grid_update_batch_size=4096` with `grid_resolution=64`.
- `grid_update_batch_size=4096` improved the selected 3-seed mean metrics over the interval-only winner: SSIM `0.555427` vs `0.554842`, LPIPS `0.425247` vs `0.428594`, and PSNR `25.729128` vs `25.698563`.
- `grid_update_batch_size=8192` had lower mean eval loss than `4096` but worse SSIM, LPIPS, and PSNR, so it is rejected for quality-oriented comparisons.
- Subsequent implementation-doubt and improvement experiments should use `grid_resolution=64`, `grid_update_interval=512`, `grid_update_batch_size=4096`, `max_res_base=2048`, `num_frequency_levels=16`, fixed ray marching, and the existing preprocessed frequency maps.
