### Frozen recipe and temporal adaptation

- All 165 cameras, eval012/095/150 and 162 train views. Long side1920, no crop;
  independent rounded-axis intrinsics scaling. Per-frame supplied calibration.
- Safe exponential density plus corrected SH, FP32 before exponential only;
  hash23/max resolution8192, 4096 rays, corrected FAS, FR.3 for temporal frames,
  no depth supervision, background optical-thickness weight.02.
- Training coarse step.001; selected export.0005 with cap4096. Render-only
  conservative train-hull occupancy envelope, as in the selected static result.
- One train-derived world normalization and one AABB for the whole motion.
  The girl extends an arm and turns during the clip; the static tight AABB cannot
  be assumed valid. Seed-frame quality is reviewed in the common bounds first.
- Reuse the existing Trainer's parameter-only temporal transfer. Only field
  parameters pass from the immediately previous real frame; optimizer, scheduler,
  scaler, RNG, occupancy, frequency state and counters start fresh. Within-frame
  extensions retain full Trainer state. AABB/normalization mismatches fail.
- Warm-frame initial LR .002, final .0001/200000-step schedule. Initial stage
  6000 with evaluation at 4096; 2000-step extensions while metrics improve,
  bounded by 24000. At a plateau, evaluate the established final render setting.
  If that passes, skip further training. If it fails, allow one 4000-step polish
  with half the LR base, then return for review if quality remains insufficient.
- Primary checkpoint selection remains all-eval PSNR, LPIPS tie-break within.07dB.
  Full-frame, foreground and moving head/body crops accompany visual review.
  Final export screening is PSNR≥30, SSIM≥.947, LPIPS≤.060, mean foreground PSNR≥24.
  Raw training candidates need PSNR≥29, SSIM≥.935, LPIPS≤.085 and foreground≥24
  before a plateau can advance to export. Poor plateaus return for review.
  Screening alone does not establish absence of artifacts.
- Final cameras remain fixed in the common world coordinates. The primary video
  shows the body; a second view shows head/upper-body detail. Planned output:
  1080×1920, 60 independently rendered frames, 30FPS, exactly2seconds.

## Insights and next steps

All frame bounds and five six-view ROI previews have been checked; shared cameras
are being framed using actual projected train-hull points over all60 frames,
with6% border clearance. The initial AABB-corner framing left too much empty space.
The seed and adjacent-frame pilots were reviewed. Inspect subsequent batches and temporal changes,
not just average metrics. Preserve raw checkpoint selection metrics separately
from denser, guarded export metrics. Final acceptance needs all-frame visual review
and verified encode duration/frame count; it cannot be inferred from a running job.

Validation so far:22 focused mask, archive and existing Trainer transfer tests pass.
Archive tests verify that a failed remote hash check cannot delete the local model
or leave a corrupt restored checkpoint available for reuse.
Five additional retention tests preserve future PSNR/LPIPS tie-window candidates
and prevent pruning checkpoints outside the current frame.
Six campaign-gate tests cover measured pilot decisions, stopping poor plateaus,
skipping unnecessary polish, budget limits and empty foreground.
Two further resume tests prevent retraining pruned stages or bypassing a rejected
visual review. MP4 encoding was checked on one real rendered frame with explicit
BT.709 matrix/range metadata and successful decode. This check is not the requested
motion video; final assembly still requires all60 models and frames.
Three exception tests require an explicit accepted review and matching checkpoint
SHA before a resumed campaign can honor an individual numeric exception.

GPU scheduling observation: simultaneous independent 2D CUDA workers reduced field
training to roughly4 iterations/s. Giving the field priority raised it to roughly37
iterations/s during fixed traversal. The coordinator temporarily suspends only this
campaign's local 2D GPU processes, retaining their state; dev3 continues. A separate
watchdog resumes them if the coordinator fails. This changes scheduling, not the
training recipe. Full process/GPU checks remain active.

The coordinator exited once when a finished frequency parent briefly remained
as a zombie process. Supervision detected it and restored scheduling. Exited
parents, reused PIDs and child-exit races are now handled; five lifecycle and
ownership tests pass. No training job or checkpoint was lost.

A four-image timing check found that one CUDA process needs 14–16 seconds per
image, versus about 7–8 seconds per image in aggregate with four processes.
Keep the four-worker queue. Repeated fits were not byte-identical; benchmark maps
remain separate and never replace campaign maps. Receipts and differences are in
`preflight/frequency_serial_benchmark/results.json`.
