### Mask holes, calibration check, and step15188

The unusually poor face095/097 PSNR is dominated by bright background retained
between the cheek and hand in the SAM3 mask. In train097, only3.1% of face-ROI
pixels have mean absolute RGB error over100/255; those pixels are chiefly leaked
background. Matching background plates confirm the cause. Existing coarse
plate-difference masks remove parts of black hair and even an eye, so they are
not safe wholesale replacements. Training inputs and primary metrics remain
unchanged. This also limits the interpretation of foreground PSNR computed with
those masks.

The provided cameras pass a separate image-based check: SIFT correspondences
consistent with an independently fitted fundamental matrix have median calibrated
epipolar distances .12–.17px for well-matched neighboring views and .30–.46px for
the tested mid-shot/close-up pairs. Poorly matched pairs150/149 and151/152 have
only ten RANSAC inliers and provide no useful calibration evidence. This test
does not prove every camera is correct, but does not support pose refinement as
the first response to the low face095/097 scores.

Supplemental face-interior boxes exclude the known background gaps. Their
coordinates are frozen in `review_luster_face_interiors.py`, applied to earlier
checkpoints too, and do not select checkpoints. They use saved8-bit PNGs.

eval29.199/.8580/.1251 (PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS). At15188 it reaches
train29.971/.8683/.1284 and eval29.889/.8630/.1108. The train decline includes a
new localized dark spot on the nose in cam097. Frozen occupancy dilation and

### Geometry cleanup ablation

A12-frame orbit at15188 confirms stable upper-body shape but a persistent skirt
halo. Black RGB supervision alone permits opaque black background geometry.
An explicit branch from the same15188 checkpoint tests background-opacity weight
.02 through30376, preserving RGB, frequency maps, FAS/FR and rays. A trusted
background pixel must have zero SAM coverage and be at least3px from any nonzero
coverage. Cam164 is excluded because its replacement mask is less reliable.
Nothing forces foreground opacity, so leaked background inside SAM is unknown
to this objective.

The objective is exact background BCE evaluated as optical thickness, the sum of
density times interval length. An initial clipped-alpha implementation was
intentionally stopped near17305 without a quality evaluation: clipping at alpha1
removes precisely the gradient needed to clear opaque ghosts. Its incomplete
trial is excluded. The corrected `exp_sh_bg02tau_30376` restarts from15188.
Fourteen focused tests cover gradients, saturated-opacity behavior, camera/mask
lookup, boundary margins and existing mixed-resolution sampling.

The first path's face framing was too wide; the final renderer derives its span
from the three fixed train-face boxes. No model parameters change when framing
the camera.

### Root cause of the persistent hem: bucket initialization

The30376 unregularized candidate reaches27.599/.9355/.0750; background supervision
reaches27.760/.9369/.0715 (PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS). It removes the cam097 nose spot and
much background opacity, but does not fix the hem. This failed visual criterion
triggered a sampling and bounds audit rather than an automatic long continuation.

Current-frame SIFT stereo finds3896 points with less than.75px reprojection error
in ten neighboring full-body pairs. All60 lower-image points are inside the
AABB. The lowest is z=-.539 versus the box floor z=-.598. Sparse correspondences
do not validate every surface point, but do not support expanding the floor.

`_initialize_buckets` retained `H_map,W_map` from the last loaded camera when
constructing every map's cell coordinates. Cam165 is landscape175×240 patches.
Portrait maps have240×175 patches: equal cell counts hid the shape error, but
their coordinates were rearranged and subsequent sampling clipped x. The largest
available portrait y was1399, so rows1400–1919 received **no RGB training rays**.
This explains the failure to learn the lower dress even after30376 steps.
The earlier ragged-collator fix preserved camera/RGB identity but did not cover
bucket initialization; its tests used manually constructed buckets.

The fix reads each map's own shape in its bucket loop. The ragged sampler also
extends the final patch's sampling range over residual border pixels, so a
1406px dimension does not omit its final six pixels. New tests cover transposed
equal-area maps, unequal-area maps, frequency labels at their original cells,
and residual-border reachability. The focused suite passes27 tests. A separate
real-data audit checks all6804000 cells exactly once with correct coordinates and
levels across162 training maps. No RGB or frequency fit is changed.

Both activation/SH arms resume from their matched15188 parents to20000 with this
fix and the same .02 background objective. This reuses learned upper-body content
while explicitly testing newly accessible image regions. First32-batch camera
coverage is saved in each run; further continuation requires reviewing the hem,
face and hands again. The old30376 branches are not extended.

## Insights and next steps

Camera orientation is mixed; treating every image as portrait would corrupt
sampling/calibration. The scene needs bounds covering the entire dress, rather
than the feature-rich region represented by the sequence's sparse-point median.
Black background is supervised RGB, not an ignored region. The early paired
gates determine whether density/SH changes transfer and whether either recipe
warrants longer training. World-space marching distances need checking when
transferring a recipe to a substantially smaller AABB.
