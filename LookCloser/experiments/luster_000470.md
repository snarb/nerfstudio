maximum observed samples per ray is 1121, with zero cap saturation. Full-resolution

### Problems diagnosed before extending training

1. **Ragged RGB gathering.** The inherited list collator sampled global FAS camera
   indices per image and overwrote them. The replacement samples once and gathers
   RGB from the selected camera; reordered/subset IDs and mixed resolutions are tested.
2. **Wrong bucket shape.** Bucket construction reused the last camera's map shape.
   Cam165 is landscape 175×240 patches; portrait maps are 240×175. Equal cell counts
   concealed the error, but portrait sampling never reached beyond row 1399.
   Rows 1400–1919 received no RGB training rays. Each map now supplies its own shape;
   residual edge pixels also remain reachable. All 6804000 cached cells are verified
   once at their correct coordinate and frequency level. Real training reaches
   rows1918–1919 and samples all 162 cameras in its first 32 batches.
3. **Marching scale.** The fight coarse step `.00625` gave roughly 75 face samples
   per ray in this smaller scene. Before 4096, training used fixed 256 while eval
   used this coarse adaptive integrator. Frozen renders removed severe streaks
   by reducing the coarse step to `.001`; allocator/cap changes did not help.
   For example, frozen step 2000 eval 150 face PSNR changed 18.129→24.815 without
   training. At 26000, export step `.0005` further improves train 151 face
   PSNR 30.727→31.053 and LPIPS`.0810`→`.0709`;`.00025` adds little.
4. **Opaque background geometry.** Black RGB alone permits black opaque floaters.
   The optional background penalty uses exact optical thickness
   `sum(sigma * delta) = -log(1-alpha)`, preserving gradients at saturated opacity.
   A clipped-alpha prototype was intentionally stopped without evaluation; its
   incomplete trial is excluded. Correct supervision removed the nose spot and
   reduced background opacity, but only the bucket fix recovered the hem.

Before the bucket fix, the 30376 candidate scored 27.599/.9355/.07496; adding the
background objective gave 27.760/.9369/.07147 but retained the distorted hem.
After the fix, only 4812 additional steps from the 15188 parent restored the skirt
and red hem. This is why those earlier branches were stopped instead of extended.

### Metric limitations from masks

SAM leaves bright background in some cheek/hand gaps, especially cam095/097.
The model correctly renders much of it black, hurting original face-ROI and
foreground PSNR. Background plates confirm the leak. Coarse plate-difference
masks also remove black hair and even an eye, so they are not wholesale replacements.
The training targets and primary evaluation remain fixed.

Supplemental face-interior boxes exclude these known gaps, are applied to earlier
checkpoints too, and do not select checkpoints. They use saved 8-bit PNGs rather
than the primary evaluator's floating RGB. Their coordinates are frozen in
`review_luster_face_interiors.py`. Calibrated feature matches show subpixel median
errors for the tested well-matched camera pairs; no pose optimization was justified.

## Insights and next steps

- Camera identity, map coordinates and full-image sampling coverage are separate
  contracts. Correct RGB gathering alone did not catch incorrect frequency buckets.
- Inspect train views and frozen rendering before interpreting blur as an
  activation problem or tuning FAS/FR. A small rendering probe isolated marching
  error; a coverage audit exposed the untrained lower dress.
- The tight AABB must enclose the full dress, not just the sparse upper-body cloud.
  Retain conservative bounds and use trusted-background supervision to clear empty space.
- Use measured `.0005` integration for final exports. Keep training-checkpoint
  selection scores and denser-export scores distinct. Final output must include
  native eval/train renders, a whole-body circle and a close face arc.
- The quiet supervisor logs controller/worker identities, progress, GPU memory and
  OOM evidence every 30 seconds. Agent checks and stage decisions are retained in
  `logs/agent_checks.jsonl` and `logs/campaign_decisions.jsonl`. Every stage stops
  for review; no detached training is left unsupervised.

Validation: original 28 implementation/checkpoint tests and tiny full-state resume
passed; the expanded focused sampler/background suite passes 27 tests, including
unequal map shapes, label positions, edge coverage and saturated-opacity gradients.
The existing LR-override state-preservation test also passes. Four render-guard
tests verify removal-only behavior, idempotence and rejection of mismatched hashes,
shapes and coordinates before mutation. Both final videos decode successfully;
all expected PNG frames and six native render inventories are present.

### Reproduce the selected export

Use `/home/brans/repos/nerfstudio/.venv/bin/python` from the isolated checkout,
with that venv and `.cuda128-toolchain/bin` on PATH, `CUDA_HOME` pointing to
`/home/brans/repos/nerfstudio/.cuda128-toolchain`, and
`TORCH_EXTENSIONS_DIR=/home/brans/.cache/torch_extensions_lookcloser`.
The user-supplied Ubuntu Conda path is unavailable on this machine.

```bash
python LookCloser/scripts/export_luster_selection.py \
  /home/brans/lookcloser_luster/000470/runs/exp_sh_fasfix_fr03_lr002_40000 \
  --output /path/to/new/export --hull-margin-voxels 3
python LookCloser/scripts/render_luster_paths.py \
  /path/to/new/export/selection.json --output /path/to/new/paths
```

The selection JSON binds the checkpoint and hashed occupancy envelope. A generic
`ns-eval` with only `config.yml` does not apply the envelope; use these scripts
for the guarded delivery. Omitting `--hull-margin-voxels` exports the raw field.
