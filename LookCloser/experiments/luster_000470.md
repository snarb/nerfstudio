## What was tested

Status: data prepared; frequency preprocessing and implementation validation in progress.
Base `09b5e830` (`lookcloser-blur-validated`), isolated branch
`lookcloser-luster-000470`. Source frame is read through `ubuntu@dev3` from
`/fsx/tmp/luster/root_8s/working/fullres/000470/`.

Local campaign: [/home/brans/lookcloser_luster/000470](/home/brans/lookcloser_luster/000470).
All 165 cameras are retained: 96 images at 1406×1920 and 69 at 1920×1406.
Eval cameras are 012, 095 and 150; the other 162 train the field. Review train
cameras are 011, 097 and 151. Images are resized with area filtering after
foreground composition. Intrinsics use actual independent width/height ratios.
There is no additional undistortion, crop or pose optimization.

Paired seed42 runs compare legacy softplus/SH with safe exponential/corrected SH.
Both use the same RGB, bounds and train-only frequency maps, legacy density units,
4096 rays, hash23, max resolution8192, FAS/FR1.0 and the leader's 4096-step warmup.
Sparse depth is disabled because 000470 has no point cloud. Neighboring clouds
are diagnostic only. The 333-file local input manifest matches remote SHA-256s.

Continuation is conditional on rendered evidence. The first paired gate is
2000, followed by 8000, 15188 and 30376 only when justified. Blurry train views
require representation/geometry/sampling diagnosis before frequency tuning.
Sharp train but blurry eval views require a view-consistency diagnosis. A
steady material improvement justifies more steps. Stage A75940 and FR0.3 through
106316 are maximum planned stages, not automatic commitments.

## Results

### Preparation and implementation

- SAM3 cam164 has 4096×2984 pixels versus the calibrated 4096×3000 RGB. The
  supplied full-size plate-difference mask replaces it; no guessed crop/stretch.
- Visual inspection identified false SAM3 components on the far-left background
  stands in cam020 and cam036. Only their source x<680 strip is cleared, with
  source files retained and a repair receipt. Neither subject overlaps the strip.
- The supplied sequence sphere is too small for the long dress. Neighboring
  sparse clouds project onto the upper body but do not densely cover the skirt.
  Bounds come from current train silhouettes, 98% voting agreement, at least ten
  witnesses, the dominant connected component and 5% per-side padding. Complete
  silhouettes constrain their camera frusta; clipped views leave out-of-frame
  regions unknown. Final bounds intersect **100% of foreground rays in all 165
  cameras**. This checks enclosing bounds, not geometric reconstruction accuracy.
- FAS's inherited ragged collator overwrote the globally sampled camera index;
  a two-image reproduction produced an out-of-bounds access. The fix samples
  once and gathers the selected image's RGB. Fifteen sampler tests pass.
- Thirteen calibration/selection/activation/SH/checkpoint tests pass. A tiny
  two-train/one-eval smoke and full-state resume completed. Smoke data uses
  synthetic constant frequency maps; its weights and scores are excluded from
  all quality comparisons.

## Insights and next steps

Camera orientation is mixed; treating every image as portrait would corrupt
sampling/calibration. The scene needs bounds covering the entire dress, rather
than the feature-rich region represented by the sequence's sparse-point median.
Black background is supervised RGB, not an ignored region. The early paired
gate will determine whether density/SH changes transfer and whether either
recipe warrants longer training.
